# RoadGuard AI — Statutory Complaint Submission Workflow

## 1. Principles & Statutory Framework

RoadGuard AI bridges citizen computer-vision detection with official Indian grievance redressal frameworks. It adheres to strict principles of **truthful reporting**, **jurisdiction-accurate routing**, and **statutory due process**.

### 1.1 Legal Grounds

Complaints generated by RoadGuard AI reference authoritative statutory provisions:

1. **Section 133, Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)**:
   * Empowers Sub-Divisional Magistrates and District Magistrates to issue conditional orders for the removal of public nuisances, hazardous road obstructions, and dangerous highway defects that jeopardize human life.
2. **Indian Roads Congress Standard IRC:82-2015**:
   * *Code of Practice for Maintenance of Bituminous Roads*: Mandates periodic road inspections, pothole rectification timelines, and pavement maintenance standards.
3. **Defect Liability Period (DLP) Provisions**:
   * Under standard MoRTH EPC contracts, concessionaires and contractors are legally bound to rectify surfacing failures within 48 to 72 hours during the concession or warranty term.

## 3. End-to-End Complaint Lifecycle

### Step 1: Defect Aggregation & Deduplication

When the YOLO11s ONNX model detects a road defect (`POTHOLE`, `LONGITUDINAL_CRACK`, `TRANSVERSE_CRACK`, `ALLIGATOR_CRACK`), the observation is spatially matched against existing `RoadIssue` entities using the Haversine formula (25-meter clustering radius).

### Step 2: Investigation & Authority Lookup

The backend `InvestigationService.java` performs reverse geocoding and highway classification to assign:

* Road Name (e.g., `NH-16 Chennai-Kolkata Highway`, `M.G. Road Vijayawada`)
* Statutory Authority (`National Highways Authority of India`, `Vijayawada Municipal Corporation`, `AP R&B Department`)
* Defect Liability Status (`DLP Applicable` vs `General Maintenance`)

### Step 3: Dossier Compilation

The complaint engine prepares a complete evidence dossier containing:

* **Complainant Profile**: Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Primary Vehicle Registration Number (VRN).
* **Location Telemetry**: Latitude, Longitude, Accuracy (meters), Road Name, Chainage/Landmark.
* **Defect Classification**: Defect type, severity, model confidence score, and timestamp.
* **Cryptographic Evidence**: Original photograph file, annotated detection preview, and SHA-256 integrity hash.
* **Legal Notice**: Formal text invoking Section 133 CrPC and IRC:82-2015.
* **Optional PDF Report**: Automatically attached summary generated by `jsPDF`.

### Step 4: Citizen Review & Edit

In the frontend `GrievanceModal.jsx`, the citizen inspects:

1. Target statutory authority and official portal URL.
2. Verified complainant contact information and vehicle registration number.
3. Formatted legal complaint narrative.
4. Thumbnail of genuine captured road evidence.
The citizen may edit the complaint narrative or attach additional remarks before proceeding.

### Step 5: Assisted Portal Handoff

Because official Indian grievance systems require one-time passwords (OTP) sent to the citizen's mobile number or BotDetect CAPTCHA evaluation, automated headless scraping is **strictly avoided**:

1. Citizen clicks **Open Official Portal & Launch Assisted Filing**.
2. RoadGuard AI copies the pre-formatted grievance narrative and coordinates to the system clipboard.
3. The official portal opens in a secure browser tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
4. The citizen enters their mobile OTP, pastes the prepared narrative, and uploads the evidence image.

### Step 6: Truthful Receipt Preservation

Upon completing the filing on the government portal:

* The citizen enters the **Official Government Acknowledgment Number** (e.g., `NHAI-2026-88192` or `AP-PGRS-2026-44012`).
* The backend permanently records the genuine reference ID, submission timestamp, and final status (`SUBMITTED_OFFICIAL`).
* If in local offline demonstration mode, records are explicitly labeled `STAGE A SIMULATED (DEMO MODE)` with clear warnings preventing confusion with official government filings.
